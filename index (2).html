最新作戦
⚠ Fresh Deployment · Sep 2026

Business Entity Resolution at Scale — Amazon ML Challenge ’26

Team ZERO · LightGBM · TF-IDF · Modal · MIT-licensed

Matching business records across three sources — 24.2 million records spanning the US, India, and France, riddled with mixed scripts, injected typos, planted look-alike records, and almost no postcodes. The pipeline: custom transliteration of nine Indic scripts with a phonetic consonant skeleton, TF-IDF blocking that recovers 95.4% of true pairs from 0.0004% of all possible pairs, and a 46-feature LightGBM classifier with per-record threshold optimization. Biggest lesson: reading errors beat tuning models — manual analysis of 25 misclassifications drove the largest single score improvement.
